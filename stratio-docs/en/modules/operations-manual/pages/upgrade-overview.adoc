= Upgrade planning

This page is a decision index, not a procedure. Use it to choose the sheet for your upgrade path and plan the maintenance window. When the intervention day arrives, see the xref:operations-manual:upgrade.adoc[upgrade procedure] to execute it step by step.

Each upgrade path has its own sheet, which indicates how long the service is out of service, what stops working, the upgrade order relative to other modules, and whether you can roll back. The sheets are written for those who plan and execute the upgrade in each environment: they gather decisions, durations, and impacts, and refer to the procedure that describes each step.

IMPORTANT: _Stratio Cloud Provisioner_ is the owner of the cluster infrastructure and of its Kubernetes version, so *its window is the first in the sequence*: that of _Stratio KEOS_ goes afterward, and those of the data modules, at the end.

TIP: Start with the summary section of the sheet. It answers, at a glance, everything you need to decide the maintenance window.

== How to read a sheet

Each sheet follows the same structure, so that the impact of each upgrade is described in the same terms:

* *The answers are brief.* Each row indicates a yes, a no, or a figure, and the last column refers to the section or page that develops it completely.
* *No field is left empty.* When something does not apply, the sheet indicates it and explains why, instead of letting you discover it on your own.
* *The detail stays in the procedure pages.* The sheet links them instead of reproducing them and leaves as "Pending" any duration that has not been measured.
